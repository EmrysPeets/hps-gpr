## Validation boundary

- The combined grid contains 232 masses from 19 through 250 MeV and exactly
  300 finite toy limits at every mass, for 69,600 finite toy limits in total.
- A common toy index joins independently drawn spectra for the datasets active
  at one mass. Different masses are independent; this is not a coherent
  scan-wide toy ensemble.
- The 415 reviewed GP states are fixed in the pseudoexperiments. The inner
  90% limit uses asymptotic `qtilde_mu` CLs with the shared-`eps2`
  `count_scale` likelihood.
- The principal combined bands use all 300 parent toys. Auxiliary standalone
  and pairwise bands select parent indices 0--99, reuse the same campaign draw
  across scopes, and contain 63,900 finite toy limits over 639 solved
  scope--mass rows.
- Pairwise bands are solved only over true two-campaign overlaps. Their
  union-range figures use the exact relevant standalone rows outside those
  overlaps, with active-set rails marking each transition.
- The bands are descriptive central quantiles conditional on the reviewed GP
  states; they are not a direct limit-coverage calibration.
- `p_strong`, `p_weak`, and bounded `p_two` are empirical fixed-mass
  observed-limit diagnostics. A raw `0/300` is below one-count resolution,
  not an exact zero probability.
- Local asymptotic discovery `p0` and the analytic resolution-spacing Sidak
  references are separate from the limit-tail diagnostics. No scan-toy
  global discovery probability is claimed.
- The full-2016 factor-12 upper length-scale setting is accepted following the
  observed v4 saturation diagnostic, so post-selection qualifications remain
  explicit.

authoritative standalone observed curve. Pairwise overlap rows retain the
accepted combined epsilon-squared root convention. Dedicated validation and
provenance JSON records are under the campaign `derived/` directory.

It retains the main limit panel and active-dataset strip, while deliberately
omitting the observed-to-median subpanel and footer. Its dedicated

The 65 MeV extraction figures are generated by
`study_results/v4p2_combined_2015full_2016full_2021_10pct_300toy_20260805/make_m065_extraction_figures.py`.
They reconstruct the exact fixed states from
`study_results/v4p1_2016_ls_upper_optimization_20260804/derived/observed_gp_states_k12_reviewed.csv`
(SHA-256
`a962c01aa030429c04e2cc102253b6b8750eacc3c9e294a7a99f851a9870aea9`)
and fail closed on the three production state hashes. The complete plotted-bin
table, fit summary, definitions, and source hashes are recorded under
`note_figures/extractions_m065/`. The figures use the simultaneous
shared-$\eps^2$ likelihood, not an inverse-variance combination of standalone
amplitudes. Their residuals are conditional fit diagnostics and are not
independent per-bin significances.
