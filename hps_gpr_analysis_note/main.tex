\begin{abstract}
This note documents the Heavy Photon Search (HPS) Gaussian-process-regression (GPR)
workflow for a narrow prompt
$A^\prime \rightarrow e^+e^-$ resonance in the HPS invariant-mass spectrum. The
analysis performs global background interpolation, local signal extraction with
Gaussian-constrained nuisance parameters for the correlated background uncertainty,
modified-frequentist
\CLs\ ($\mathrm{CL}_{s+b}/\mathrm{CL}_b$) upper limits in signal-yield and
$\eps^2$ space, and a statistically independent multi-dataset combination in a shared
$\eps^2$ parameter of interest. The signal model is implemented as a full-range
Gaussian template whose blinded-bin slice enters the local likelihood without
renormalization, so the fitted amplitude retains the interpretation of a total local
signal yield. The note also summarizes the radiative-fraction normalization,
length-scale parameterization, toy-validation program, and staged path from the
published 2015 and 2016 HPS prompt searches toward a fully unblinded combination.
Version 4 separates the mass interval searched for a signal from the wider spectrum
used to train each GP. Version 4.1 kept those supports and revisited one numerical
range after the v4 observed scan showed that all 142 full-2016 fits saturated the
resolution-scaled length-scale ceiling. A controlled scan of
$k_{\max,2016}=8,10,12,15,$ and 20 identified 12 as the first nonbinding value
followed by a stable plateau. Version 4.2 accepts that factor-12 setting and completes
the 90\% confidence-level combined search using the full 2015 and 2016 samples and
the reviewed 2021 10\% sample. The principal result is the shared-$\eps^2$ observed
asymptotic \CLs{} limit with central expected intervals from exactly 300
background-only joint pseudoexperiments per mass. A common pseudoexperiment index is
shared by every dataset active at that mass. The three constituent distributions
and the three pairwise combinations are additionally documented with auxiliary
100-pseudoexperiment conditional limit bands that reuse the first 100 entries of
the accepted parent stream. Local asymptotic $p_0$ values are shown separately.
The observed 65~MeV local minimum is
documented with an exact fixed-state extraction, sideband context, profiled
residuals, and a comparison of the standalone and shared-coupling estimates.
An August 6 addendum adds an observed-equivalent BaBar comparison, exact
fixed-mass common-binning and physical-profile checks, and two fixed-seed
conditional central-window replacements. These diagnostics do not alter the
accepted v4.2 card or principal result and introduce no projected bands,
coverage statement, or scan-calibrated global claim.
A ten-toy, five-scenario 2021 source-and-exposure screen is included only as a
fit-space pilot of the hyperparameter-closure machinery. The completed
background-only audit makes factor 20 the provisional projected-100\% ceiling, while
factor 25 is required if one common ceiling is imposed on every pilot exposure.
The fixed-amplitude screen shows no loss of local extraction sensitivity at factor 20.
Its common 2--3\% under-response is already present at factors 15 and 25 and is retained
as an unresolved ten-toy closure diagnostic, not a bias claim. The pilot constructs no
limit bands or coverage result and does not qualify the observed result;
production-matched hyperparameter closure and direct limit coverage remain required
before interpreting the conditional bands as coverage-calibrated intervals. The
300 mass-local pseudoexperiments do not calibrate a scan-wise global discovery
$p$-value.
\end{abstract}
