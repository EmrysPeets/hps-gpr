\section{Conclusions and Outlook}
\label{sec:conclusions}

This note documents a coherent GPR-based framework for the HPS narrow prompt
$A^\prime \rightarrow e^+e^-$ search. The workflow now ties the detector-era inputs,
mass-resolution parameterizations, radiative-fraction normalization, log-count GP
background model, local profiled signal extraction, \CLs\ limit setting, and
shared-$\eps^2$ combination into one reviewable chain.

The validation suite supports the current blind-window and extraction choices with
several complementary studies. The training-exclusion scan shows why the GP sidebands
should begin outside approximately $2.25\,\sigma_m$ rather than immediately outside a
narrow $1.64\,\sigma_m$ blind region. The refmatched 2015 blind-geometry comparison
then shows that a $2.25\,\sigma_m$ blind/extraction window gives similar closure to the
$1.64\,\sigma_m$ blind plus $2.25\,\sigma_m$ guard construction while retaining better
expected reach. The length-scale lower-bound scan motivates the selected $1\sigma$
stability bound, and the functional-form seed panels document the analytic backgrounds
used for the smooth-spectrum toy ensembles. The dataset-level refmatched full-refit
closure panels for 2015, 2016 10\%, and 2021 1\% document the corresponding pull
calibration and residual significance calibration.

The current staged internal-review result is the corrected
2015$+$2016 10\%$+$2021 1\% simultaneous shared-$\eps^2$ analysis. The individual
dataset limits and local $p_0$ scans are shown as diagnostic inputs, but the combined
limit is obtained from the joint profiled likelihood rather than from an envelope of
single-dataset curves. The observed extraction display at \SI{119}{MeV} provides a
concrete check of how the dataset-level fits enter the shared-coupling result.
The 90\% CLs interpretation now also includes the dimuon-threshold branching
correction and a dedicated 2021 target-constrained resolution-smearing comparison.
That comparison shows that broadening the 2021 signal template redistributes the
local discovery structure: some mass regions become more signal-like, while several
prominent unscaled excess-like features become less significant.

The full-statistics curves remain projections. They are useful for comparing the
expected reach of the GPR strategy with the published HPS prompt result and the BaBar
visible-dark-photon contour, but they are not observed full-statistics exclusions. The
local/global significance overlays shown in the note are likewise review diagnostics;
any final discovery statement should use a frozen scan-level toy calibration of the
maximum discovery statistic.

The remaining work is well bounded. The final production pass should freeze the full
2016 and 2021 inputs, finalize the radiative-fraction and systematic-penalty model,
regenerate the complete toy-tail and scan-level look-elsewhere calibration tables, and
rerender the note figures from the same production outputs used for the final
unblinding. With those pieces in place, the framework documented here is ready to
support the first fully combined HPS GPR prompt-resonance result.
