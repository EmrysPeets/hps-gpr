\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

Version 4.2 adopts the v4.1 factor-12 upper length-scale setting for the full
2016 sample and completes the corresponding three-campaign result. The full 2015
and 2016 samples and the reviewed 2021 10\% sample enter one shared-$\eps^2$
likelihood. Relative to v4, only the 2016 resolution-scaled length-scale upper
factor changes, from 8 to 12; the data, mass grids, fit supports, blind and
training-exclusion geometry, lower factors, likelihood, and 90\% asymptotic
\CLs{} construction are unchanged.

The v4.2 products comprise the combined observed limit and its principal
300-pseudoexperiment conditional ensemble, the three campaign-level observed
limits, auxiliary 100-pseudoexperiment standalone and pairwise conditional
ensembles, and fixed-mass asymptotic $p_0$ scans. The auxiliary bands are
evaluated from the reviewed campaign states and are not reassigned from the
three-campaign ensemble.

\subsection{Controlled 2016 upper-range study}
\label{sec:results-v4p1-lsupper-study}
\label{sec:results-v4-support-audit}

The study evaluates $k_{\max,2016}=8,10,12,15,$ and 20 on the same 142 hypotheses
from 39 to 180~MeV. A state is classified as upper-bound occupied when
$\ell_{\mathrm{opt}}/\ell_{\max}\geq0.999$. The occupancy falls from 142 of 142 at
factor 8 to 56 of 142 at factor 10 and zero at factors 12, 15, and 20.

\label{fig:results-v4p1-lsupper-study}
\end{figure}

Questionable branches were rerun with the data and card held fixed. Across the four
new factor scans, 14 selected repair rows use the largest log marginal likelihood
among actual repeated fits, and each selected row is independently reproduced. No
limit, significance, or GP coordinate is interpolated. From factor 12 to 15, the
largest pointwise change in the 2016 observed yield upper limit is 0.083\%, the
largest $|\Delta Z|$ is 0.00255, and the log-marginal-likelihood differences lie in
$[-4.5,8.0]\times10^{-6}$. The factor-15 to factor-20 comparison is similarly
stable. These results define factor 12 as the first nonbinding plateau setting.
Neither the direction of the observed-limit change nor the local $p_0$ values
enter that rule.

The ten-toy 2021 source-and-exposure screen in
Section~\ref{sec:v4p1-ls-exposure-pilot} is not part of this selection rule. It is
a later fit-space pilot and supplies neither an expected band nor a coverage
qualification or trials correction for the observed factor scan. None of the
observed limits or asymptotic $p_0$ values below is conditioned on a conclusion
drawn from those ten toys.

\subsection{Exact reconstruction and conditional ensemble}
\label{sec:results-v4p2-reconstruction}

The reviewed state ledger contains exactly 415 campaign--mass GP states: 72 for
2015, 142 for 2016, and 201 for 2021. The final runner reconstructs the GP mean
and covariance at those exact optimized coordinates and evaluates the shared
\texttt{count\_scale} likelihood on the union of 232 mass hypotheses. Every
reported limit and local asymptotic $p_0$ is evaluated at an actual fitted state;
no mass point, GP coordinate, limit, or significance is interpolated.

The combined expected distribution uses 300 conditional background-only
pseudoexperiments at each mass. Within a mass, a common toy index joins the
campaign-specific draws entering the simultaneous likelihood. Different masses
are generated independently, so these are mass-local limit pseudoexperiments,
not 300 scan-wide toy spectra. Each pseudoexperiment holds the reviewed GP
hyperparameters fixed, without a GP refit, and uses the same asymptotic inner
90\% \CLs{} construction as the observed limit. This ensemble supports
descriptive combined-limit quantiles and mass-local limit-tail comparisons; it
does not establish direct \CLs{} coverage or a scan-wise discovery probability.

\subsection{Standalone and pairwise conditional limit ensembles}
\label{sec:results-v4p2-standalone-pairwise-bands}

An auxiliary ensemble documents the expected fixed-state limit distribution for
each standalone campaign and each of the three pairwise shared-$\eps^2$
likelihoods. At every mass, it reconstructs the accepted v4.2
300-pseudoexperiment parent stream and selects toy indices 0--99. The same
campaign-specific pseudo-spectrum is therefore reused whenever that campaign
appears at a common toy index. The 415 standalone campaign--mass rows and 224
genuine two-campaign overlap rows contain exactly 100 finite upper limits each,
for 63,900 evaluated toy limits. The reviewed GP state is held fixed in every
pseudoexperiment.

\subsection{Observed extraction at the 65 MeV local minimum}

The comparison shows that the localized feature is predominantly carried by the
2021 10\% sample and is modestly supported by 2015. The 2016 spectrum does not
favor the signal yield implied by the common coupling and lowers the

absolute shared-fit Pearson residual in any one displayed bin is approximately

apparent localized excess on the selected fixed card. They do not supply a
scan-toy global significance, and the 300 mass-local limit pseudoexperiments do
not calibrate such a claim.

\subsection{Relation to v4 and validation boundary}
\label{sec:results-v4p2-v4-context}

Version 4.2 accepts factor 12 as the full-2016 numerical setting and reports the
completed fixed-GP result on that card. The 300-pseudoexperiment combined
ensemble and auxiliary 100-pseudoexperiment standalone and pairwise ensembles
qualify their plotted conditional quantiles, while only the former is used for
the reported mass-local combined limit-tail fractions. They do not
retroactively turn the observed-data range study into a pre-unblinding choice.
They also do not provide direct \CLs{} coverage, a GP-refit ensemble, or a
scan-wise maximum-$q_0$ calibration. Those qualifications remain separate
requirements for a coverage or global discovery claim.
