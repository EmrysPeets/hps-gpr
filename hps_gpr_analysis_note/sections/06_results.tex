\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

This section presents the July 8, 2026 pre-unblinding candidate used for the v3
internal review. The physics quantity is a 90\% confidence-level upper limit obtained
with the modified-frequentist \CLs{} construction. Results are shown in signal-yield
space for the corrected 2021 sample and in $\eps^2$ space for both 2021 and the
simultaneous three-campaign fit. Older 95\% studies, superseded kernel choices, and
the detailed history of earlier plotting studies are collected in
Appendix~\ref{app:prior-validation-results}.

\subsection{Pre-unblinding candidate configuration}
\label{sec:results-frozen-candidate}

The candidate combines the full 2015 sample, the 2016 10\% sample, and the corrected
2021 1\% sample in a shared-$\eps^2$ likelihood. The 2021 input is
\path{final_1pct_invM.root}, histogram \path{preselection/h_invM_8000}, with events
satisfying $p_{\mathrm{sum}}<\SI{2.8}{GeV}$ rejected, no vertex-fit
$\chi^2$ requirement, and the electron-track hit requirement loosened to at least
eight two-dimensional hits. The configuration is summarized in
Table~\ref{tab:results-v3-configuration}.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\caption{Configuration frozen for the July 8, 2026 pre-unblinding candidate. The
radiative-fraction penalties are fixed normalization shifts in the conversion to
$\eps^2$; they are not profiled nuisance parameters in the limit fit.}
\label{tab:results-v3-configuration}
\begin{tabular}{lccccc}
\toprule
Campaign & Scan range [MeV] & Data sample & $k_{\min}$ & $k_{\max}$ &
radiative penalty \\
\midrule
2015 & 19--90  & full & 1.0 & 8 & 7.0\% \\

2021 & 50--250 & 1\%  & 1.1 & 9 & 4.6\% \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

Here $k_{\min}$ and $k_{\max}$ multiply the year-specific spacing scale
$\sigma_x$ in the log-mass coordinate. The combined likelihood uses the
\texttt{count\_scale} simultaneous mode, the 2021 target-constrained mass-resolution
parameterization, and 10,000 background-only expected-limit toys at each evaluated
mass hypothesis. The simultaneous curve is not an envelope of the individual limits:
one common $\eps^2$ value predicts a different signal yield in each active campaign
through the corresponding conversion factor.
For the expected-band ensemble, the GP mean and covariance trained on the observed
sidebands are held fixed in every pseudoexperiment
(\texttt{bands\_refit\_gp\_on\_toy=False}). The resulting bands therefore calibrate
the conditional local likelihood; they do not include the additional variation that
would arise from retraining the GP on every toy spectrum.

The source scans contain a small number of isolated numerical failures. In the clean
review figures, the observed individual limits at 2015 24~MeV and 2016 44, 98, 133,
and 171~MeV are replaced by linear interpolation in the logarithm of the limit between
the adjacent 1~MeV hypotheses. The simultaneous 136~MeV limit is repaired by the same
rule and the resulting value, $6.93625\times10^{-6}$, is used consistently in the
combined-limit, comparison, and projection figures. The 2021-only 136~MeV yield and
coupling entries use the same adjacent-mass rule in Figure~\ref{fig:results-v3-2021-pair}.
At 24~MeV, where only 2015 is active, the repaired 2015 value is propagated to the
simultaneous table so that the individual and simultaneous limits agree exactly. The
unmodified values, both neighbors, and every replacement are retained in the
accompanying audit table. These
are presentation repairs for committee review, not substitutes for production reruns
of the affected mass hypotheses. The inherited expected-band tables retain log-linear
spike repairs at 37, 115, and 136~MeV in the simultaneous table and at 79, 173, 181,
and 217~MeV in the 2021-only table. These expected-band replacements are likewise
display-level quantities whose production values must be regenerated.

\subsection{Simultaneous 2015, 2016, and 2021 limit}
\label{sec:results-combined-151621-1pct}

The principal v3 review figure is the direct simultaneous shared-$\eps^2$ limit in
Figure~\ref{fig:results-v3-combined-band}. The active campaigns change with mass

minimal-model branching correction for $A^\prime\rightarrow\mu^+\mu^-$ above threshold.

\subsection{Local and ensemble p-value diagnostics}
\label{sec:results-discovery-diagnostics}

The fixed-mass discovery statistic and the upper-limit consistency tests answer
different questions and are shown separately. The local $p_0$ scan tests a positive
signal at each fixed mass. The upper-limit tests instead locate the observed upper
limit within the corresponding background-only upper-limit ensemble. Stored toy-tail
fractions at 24, 37, 44, 59, 91, 98, 115, 133, 136, 159, and 166~MeV were evaluated
against observed limits that were later superseded in the display table. Those rows
cannot be recomputed from the saved quantiles alone, so they are omitted rather than
assigned interpolated p-values; the curves connect the adjacent valid hypotheses.

\subsection{Projection to the full 2016 and 2021 datasets}
\label{sec:results-full-projections}

No earlier projection used the selected $k_{\min}=(1.0,0.9,1.1)$ configuration.
Figure~\ref{fig:results-v3-projection} therefore applies the note's density-scaling
convention directly to the July 8 table. At each mass the current local density terms
are reweighted by factors of 1 for the full 2015 sample, 10 for the 2016 10\% sample,
and 100 for the 2021 1\% sample. The projected coupling limit is
\begin{equation}
s(m)=
\sqrt{\frac{\sum_d \rho_d(m)}
{\sum_d f_d\,\rho_d(m)}} ,
\qquad
\eps^2_{\mathrm{proj}}(m)=s(m)\,\eps^2_{\mathrm{current}}(m),
\end{equation}
where $f_d$ is the campaign scaling factor and the sums include only campaigns active
at that mass.

\begin{figure}[H]
\centering
\graphicorplaceholder{0.92\linewidth}{final_limit_projection_figs/v3_20260709_final_preunblind/final_preunblind_current_vs_full_campaign_projection_eps2.png}
\caption{Current July 8 candidate and the density-scaled projection to the full 2016
and 2021 datasets. The full 2015 sample is unchanged; the 2016 10\% and 2021 1\%
density terms are multiplied by 10 and 100. The curves are drawn continuously across
changes in the set of active campaigns. This comparison is a planning estimate based
on the current observed and expected-median curves, not a refit to the full datasets
or a production expected-sensitivity calculation.}
\label{fig:results-v3-projection}
\end{figure}

Before the result is promoted beyond internal review, the documented failed mass
hypotheses must be rerun and the expected bands regenerated without display-level
interpolation. Direct \CLs{} coverage should then be verified with the frozen
production configuration. The \v{S}id\'ak curves provide the scan-level global
approximation used for the present review.
