\subsection{Frozen scope and cardinality}

Each of the $1\%\times10$ and native-10\% source families contributes 25 independent
Poisson spectra generated over 30--300~MeV.  Both the 30--300 and 40--300~MeV GP
supports are applied to the same cached counts.  Four masses (55, 60, 65, and
70~MeV) and four signed injection strengths ($z=0,1,3,5$) give
$2\times25\times4\times4=800$ states per support and 1600 total.  The supports have
identical key inventories.  Source families are independent; masses, injections, and
supports within a source family are correlated through their shared backgrounds.

The analysis preserves the frozen v4.2/v4.5 settings other than the support lower
edge.  No expected-limit toys, observed limits, exclusions, or scan-maximum
calibrations are produced.

\subsection{Source-truth construction and adverse gate}

The executed truth label is
\texttt{fSigPowExpQ-anchored residual stress truth}.  For each source, a degree-six
logistic-times-exponentiated-Chebyshev model is fitted on 30--80~MeV.  A fixed $C^2$
blend from 75 to 85~MeV joins that local component to an identifiable
\texttt{fSigPowExpQ} anchor, which supplies the tail through 300~MeV.  The truth is
positive and exactly normalized to the corresponding 30--300~MeV source total.

The local native-bin deviance/ndf values are
\VFourPNineLocalDevianceOnePct{} and \VFourPNineLocalDevianceNativeTen{} for the 1\%
and native-10\% sources.  Those values do not override the adverse global checks: the
native-10\% 85--300~MeV tail deviance/ndf is
\VFourPNineTailDevianceNativeTen{}, and the largest leave-one-$2.25\sigma_m$-window-
out drift is \VFourPNineMaximumLOODrift.  All sixteen pure
\texttt{fSigPowExpQ}/\texttt{fGenGammaThresh} fits over the predeclared 30/40 to
100/150~MeV ranges have \texttt{fit\_ok=false}.  Thus neither a pure simple family nor
the executed joined truth is promoted as an independent globally qualified generator.

\subsection{Allowed claims}

The study establishes a statistically resolved, same-toy dependence of the 65~MeV
conditional pull on the GP lower support edge.  It also establishes that the change is
not sufficient for uniform closure under the executed stress truth.  It does not show
that the observed 65~MeV feature is background, that GPR coverage is calibrated, or
that the 30--300~MeV production support is qualified.

The proposed restricted-range asterisk is retained only as guidance for a future
study: a threshold-faithful, source-qualified function evaluated on 30--100 or
30--150~MeV could demonstrate a local threshold-modeling requirement without changing
the observed-data support.  No restricted toy ensemble was executed in v4.9, and the
simple families tested on those ranges failed before extraction.
