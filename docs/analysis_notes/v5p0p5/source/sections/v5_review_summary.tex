\section{Status for the full-data unblinding review}
\label{sec:v5-review}

The analysis combines the full 2015 and 2016 datasets with the available
2021 10\% sample. A common coupling relates the signal yields while each
campaign retains its own normalization, mass resolution and background
model. The injection studies support profiling the background uncertainty
rather than fixing the predicted mean.

The calibration studies identify background shapes for which the asymptotic
procedure rejects the null hypothesis too often. Large interpolation offsets
can also drive the most extreme global probabilities without a large positive
signal in the observed spectrum. These results make the choice and validation
of the background model central to interpreting the probabilities. The
calibration results are discussed in Appendix~\ref{v5-sec:calibration};
the numerical checks are collected in
Appendix~\ref{sec:v5-historical-validation}.

Candidate masses, template widths, mass ranges and background alternatives
should be fixed before examining additional 2021 data. Any staged comparison
should examine the new events separately as well as in combination with the
current sample, since the combined sample still contains the original
fluctuation.

The 2015 extension through 100~MeV includes conditional toy studies and an
updated combined scan. It does not establish coverage for every background
shape. Where the number of toys is insufficient to resolve a tail probability,
the figures retain a Monte Carlo upper bound.
