\section{Status for the full-data unblinding review}
\label{sec:v5-review}

The current analysis gives a reproducible set of observed limits and local
asymptotic diagnostics for full 2015, full 2016 and the released 2021 10\%
sample. The common-coupling construction respects each campaign's signal
normalization, resolution and background constraint. Conditional injection
studies support retaining the profiled background rather than fixing its
predicted mean exactly. The later studies also make clear where the apparent
precision of a local fit exceeds what the background validation establishes.

Three issues remain material to review. First, the 2016 state archive retains
the disclosed cross-process reproduction exception; downstream prediction
checks do not erase it. Second, the calibration study finds substantial
rejection-frequency discrepancies for the raw asymptotic procedure under some
declared generating backgrounds. Third, the most extreme GP global tails can
be driven by large offsets in those background constructions, rather than by
large positive observed signals. Those findings require a qualified background
family and a declared final calibration choice before the corresponding
probabilities acquire a final physics interpretation.

The additional data should test choices fixed using the currently released
sample. Candidate masses, template widths, background controls, mass ranges,
analysis variants and any sequential data looks need to be recorded before
examining the new events. A staged increment can be assessed separately from
its cumulative combination so that persistence is not inferred only from a
sample containing the original fluctuation. This note does not prescribe or
perform an additional data release; it provides the analysis record for that
decision.

The remaining editorial and computational tasks are tracked in the accompanying
handoff. In particular, unresolved Monte Carlo tails have retained bounds,
unqualified 2016/2021 global results have explicit status statements. The v5.0.4 extension adds conditional toys at the ten new 2015 masses
and recomputes the affected combined field; it introduces no new observed
events. The 90--100~MeV interval is included with that stated validation
status, rather than a claim of newly established coverage.
