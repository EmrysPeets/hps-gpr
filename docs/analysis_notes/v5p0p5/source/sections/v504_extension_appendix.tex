\section{The 2015 extension from 90 to 100 MeV}
\label{app:v504-extension}
The extension introduced in v5.0.4 and retained in v5.0.5 includes 2015 at every integer hypothesis from 19 to 100~MeV.
The 2015+2016 overlap is 39--100~MeV; 2015+2021 and all three span
50--100~MeV. The complete 19--250~MeV search therefore uses all three
campaigns at 50--100~MeV and 2016+2021 at 101--180~MeV. Dataset membership
follows these intervals, without selecting the more favorable observed curve.

The spectrum, binning, 14--135~MeV GP support, normalization prescription
and original mass-resolution model are retained. Above 90~MeV the two
kernel parameters are held at their archived 90~MeV values. Each actual
mass has its own moving $\pm2.25\sigma_m$ mask, template, density conversion,
background prediction, likelihood and upper limit. This is the endpoint
prescription already used by the v5.1.1 covariance pilot and v5.2.1 fine scan;
the independently refitted width-nuisance studies are not substituted for it.
The archived 85~MeV kernel remains a separate endpoint control, rather than a
choice made from favorable observed limits.

The inherited linear resolution gives $\sigma_m(90)=4.6975$~MeV and
$\sigma_m(100)=5.2297$~MeV, an increase of 11.3\%, not a constant width.
At 100~MeV the training exclusion is approximately 88.23--111.77~MeV,
well inside the existing support, and the density window is also contained.
This supports a modest continuation of the same analysis geometry. It does
not independently measure the extrapolated resolution or establish coverage.
The main figures shade 90--100~MeV to make this extension identifiable.

\subsection{Targeted conditional calculations}
The observed integer-mass limits at 91--100~MeV reuse the saved v5.2.1
fixed-endpoint fits. An exact numerical replay of every added mass and scope
checks those inputs before the bands are computed. There are 300 new
background-only experiments at each of the ten masses, separately for each
campaign. A correlated Gaussian draw around the frozen local GP prediction
is followed by Poisson counts. Constituent counts are shared among the four
2015-containing scopes at a mass. These 9,000 constituent draws yield
12,000 fitted limits, with maximum $|CL_s-0.1|=1.05\times10^{-8}$.
The likelihood profiles the background; the GP prediction is held fixed.
No latent draw was clipped. Quantiles and counts are saved by mass, scope
and toy identifier. These mass-local ensembles provide expected-limit bands
and upper-limit tail diagnostics; they are not coherent scan experiments.
The pre-extension 300-toy bands and targeted tail batches remain unchanged.
New empty 300-toy tails retain the one-sided bound $p<0.00994$ at 95\%
Monte Carlo confidence. No empty empirical cell is set to a finite discovery
probability by extrapolation.

The saved 95~MeV injection pilot provides a distinct recovery check: 64
paired spectra per truth and injection strength $0,2,5$ reference errors,
under both a local GP control and the coherent stress spectrum. It also
tests deterministic signal response over the enlarged grid. These checks
support the stated conditional continuation; they do not turn the extension
into an independently calibrated coverage result.

For the updated combined field, all ten changed mass coordinates are
recomputed using the actual shared-coupling likelihood. The input-bin
response has 1,626 directions. The 90~MeV bridge reproduces the archived
deterministic roots to $1.88\times10^{-6}$, and exact direct refits check
the rank-one GP updates used for single-bin perturbations. The same first
256 archived joint Poisson spectra are retained across all 232 hypotheses,
including the changed membership. The 2015 field uses all 82 masses and
its same 256 coherent spectra. Each updated field is integrated with
200,000 Gaussian draws; positive observed fits with standardized score
at least 3.5 receive 40,000 conditional-mixture draws. This predetermined
rule improves Monte Carlo precision under the given Gaussian model. Its
small tail intervals do not include error in the stress background or in
the Gaussian approximation. A zero direct count in 256 experiments gives
only $p<0.01163$ at 95\% Monte Carlo confidence.

\subsection{Trials factors and what changes in the result}
The resolution-spacing Sidak estimate for 2015 increases from 13.624 to
14.521. The 2015+2016 value becomes 10.011; 2015+2021 and all three each
give 9.948. The complete-search value stays at 35.381 because the rule
uses the narrowest active detector resolution, already supplied by the
other campaigns in the added interval. Thus extending dataset membership
does not necessarily enlarge this particular approximation to the trials
factor. It still has no correction for selecting among overlapping scopes.

The complete search's strongest nominal local excess remains at 66~MeV,
with $Z=2.765$ and Sidak reference $p=0.0959$. At 92~MeV the new
three-campaign result is $Z=2.520$ with $p_0=0.00586$; the separate-rate
comparison in Section~\ref{sec:v5-rate-consistency} is needed to interpret
what the common fit describes. The extension is made after the original
data have been examined, and its conditional studies do not supply a
selection-adjusted discovery significance or new unblinding authorization.
