# HPS-GPR v5 study and analysis-note archive

This archive preserves the distinct completed PDF versions available on 16 September 2026. It includes the standalone studies, prior analysis-note deliveries and their separately delivered figure PDFs. Original PDF bytes are unchanged. `manifest.json` records their SHA-256 identities and original paths; identical copies are stored once with aliases. Transient build/QA PDFs are not additional study versions.

The consolidated analysis note is now **v5.0.5**; [its source, checked PDF and revision notes](../../analysis_notes/v5p0p5/README.md) identify the material integrated from these studies. Study numbers label separate investigations, not successive replacements of the entire note. In particular, v5.5.4, v5.6.1 and v5.7.1 remain the citations for the standalone work.

None of these archive operations reruns or upgrades a historical result. Local probabilities, selected alternatives, conditional toy projections, Gaussian-reference tails and geometric fractions retain the qualifications in their reports. The new v5.0.5 joint binning check is distinct from the original v5.1.0 individual scans.

## Relationships that matter when reading the reports

- **v5.5.0 → v5.5.4:** mass-scaling tests lead to fixed-mass empirical rate comparisons, transfer checks and a common experiment for shape/rate fits. The final report adds parameter covariance and separates fitting count spectra from fitting signed extracted amplitudes. A fitted residual energy factor is not a measured acceptance or production law.
- **v5.6.0 → v5.6.1:** the injected spectra and 300 Poisson draws are the same. v5.6.1 adds the upper-limit response across the scan. The 1% and 10% sources stay separate, and target matching is not evidence that a present fluctuation must persist.
- **v5.7.0 → v5.7.1:** the 70 mrad angular surrogate is replaced by finite sensor geometry and magnetic transport. The result still omits production-spectrum integration, material, reconstruction and trigger efficiencies.
- **v5.3 → v5.3.2:** deterministic width scenarios become individual and then combined nuisance fits. The assumed width constraints are not measured calibration uncertainties, and no global mass/width-search calibration is added.
- **v5.2.2 PDFs:** the shifted-pair report, triplet-only extension and complete combined document are separate retained products under one study version.
- **Repeated v5.0.2/v5.0.4 filenames:** parent directories distinguish the original, audit/support and figure revisions. Do not identify a result by version number alone; use the file hash.
