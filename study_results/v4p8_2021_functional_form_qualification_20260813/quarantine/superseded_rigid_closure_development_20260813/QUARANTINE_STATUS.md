# Superseded rigid-closure development runs

These directories are excluded from the authoritative v4p8p2 result.  They
contain earlier one-lane development attempts under superseded contracts.  In
particular, `runs/rigid_closure/2021_1pct_x10/` contains completed outputs for
toy indices 20, 21, and 22.  Those indices were therefore inspected before the
frozen 0--19 analysis partition and must not be described as an unopened
statistical reserve.

The authoritative closure products are under
`runs/rigid_closure_v4p8p2_20toy_frozen/` and
`derived/rigid_closure_v4p8p2_20toy_frozen/`.  The authoritative pull-blind
length products are under `runs/rigid_length_scan/` and
`derived/rigid_length_scan/`.  Neither authoritative workflow consumes an
index above 19.
