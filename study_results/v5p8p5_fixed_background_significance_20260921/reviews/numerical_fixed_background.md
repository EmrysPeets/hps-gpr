# Numerical implementation and provenance audit

The requested fixed-background scan completed on the full 0.5 MeV grid for the observed spectra and all 256 coherent toy replicates. The calculation took 559.09 seconds. `results/completion.json` records 463 union-grid nodes, 1,773 observed scope/mass rows, 1,975 reported scope/domain/mass rows, and seven scope/domain peak summaries. Dataset ranges are 2015: 19–100 MeV (163 nodes), 2016: 39–180 MeV (283 nodes), and 2021: 50–250 MeV (401 nodes). Shared-coupling and free-amplitude combinations cover the union 19–250 MeV, with a separately declared all-three overlap of 50–100 MeV. Observed and toy global maxima use exactly the same grid and domain.

## Implementation

`scripts/fixed_core.py` preserves the parent moving ±2.25σ mask, kernel choices, signal templates, and GP arithmetic count mean, `b = exp(mu + diag(C_log)/2)`. For each observed, Asimov, or toy spectrum it recomputes the masked GP conditioning, including count-dependent training noise. Computing only the posterior covariance diagonal removes unnecessary matrix construction; it does not change the predicted count mean.

The extraction likelihood is a product of Poisson probabilities with expectation `b + A S`. The fitted background `b` is fixed during each amplitude optimization: there is no GP covariance nuisance and no profiling of its uncertainty. A safeguarded scalar Newton solve, with Brent fallback, maximizes the likelihood subject to positive bin expectations. The code records the signed likelihood root, one-sided positive statistic, amplitude, curvature error, numerical score, and smallest fitted bin expectation. `A = epsilon² / 10⁻⁸`; signed estimates may be negative. Curvature errors describe this C=0 likelihood, not validated repeated-sampling coverage.

The shared fit concatenates the active datasets' original templates without renormalizing them and uses one amplitude. Free nonnegative amplitudes factorize exactly: `q_R = sum_d max(r_d, 0)²`. Individual/shared nominal local tails use the standard Gaussian root reference; free nominal tails use the central K-amplitude chi-bar-square mixture. These are nominal references, not empirically validated probabilities. Inclusive p=1 is recorded at the zero-statistic atom, alongside the conventional half-chi-square map for individual/shared fits.

The primary free global ordering is the maximum negative logarithm of the nominal local mixture p-value, which makes the changing active-dataset count explicit. A separate raw maximum-q result is also saved. Every local and global empirical count applies the identical statistic to observed and toy curves. There is no reference recentering, rescaling, or fitted significance-GP simulation.

## Provenance and reproduction

The portable parent engine and pinned inputs were copied into `inputs/parent/` from `study_results/v5p8p5_consolidated_fairness_20260921/inputs/v5p8p2_nominal_gp_significance_20260917/`. The copied spectra agree exactly with the observed arrays in their corresponding null files. `results/protocol.json` records input SHA-256 hashes and scientific conventions; the package QA additionally inventories the parent inputs.

The toy spectra are the original 256 complete Poisson spectra per dataset in `inputs/parent/inputs/null_YEAR.npz`. Their `truth` arrays are the frozen nominal all-data GP sources. Reusing these spectra preserves coherent mass-to-mass fluctuations and permits paired comparison with the parent calculation. These are new C=0 refits of that existing bank, not an additional independent bank. There are 847 dataset/mass GP contexts and 216,832 toy GP predictions, plus observed and source-Asimov predictions.

Run `scripts/run_fixed_scan.py` with `/Applications/Xcode.app/Contents/Developer/usr/bin/python3`. The script fixes scientific numerical libraries to one thread, suppresses bytecode writes into the parent engine, checks the study `STOP` marker, and checkpoints each mass separately. `results/fields_SCOPE.npz` retain complete observed/toy curves, `results/toy_maxima.npz` retains domain maxima, and `results/significance_curves.csv` includes conditional local and global tails at every reported mass, including all requested anchors. `results/peaks.json` contains the seven peak summaries and the alternate free maximum-q results.

## Verification and claim limits

Root's independent checks are recorded in `qa/independent_numerical.json` and `qa/scan_validation.json`: parent full-covariance GP means, 49 selected inherited scalar-likelihood fits, complete-scan tail counts, likelihood nesting/factorization, input hashes, score convergence, and positive expectations passed. The largest selected signed-root difference from the independent solver was approximately 7.9×10⁻¹³. The largest observed scaled score in the completion record was 1.98×10⁻⁸. No additional fit rerun was required after these checks.

Empirical results remain conditional on the frozen parent source and the inherited source-construction procedure. Source uncertainty and alternative background truths are not propagated. With 256 toys, zero exceedances are finite-sample upper bounds, not zero probabilities; the tables preserve counts, two-sided 95% Clopper–Pearson intervals, one-sided 95% upper bounds, and the optional add-one estimate. A peak selected from a scan does not acquire an independent fixed-mass interpretation from its local tail; the declared domain-global count addresses that search.

Large C=0 nominal roots cannot be read directly as physical significances. For example, at the all-three shared 66 MeV anchor the source-Asimov root is 0.0602 but the toy signed roots have mean −0.0599 and standard deviation 2.7299, with 59/256 nominal 5% rejections. This demonstrates a local fluctuation-scale failure before any look-elsewhere correction. The union shared peak at 22 MeV includes only 2015; it must not be described as an all-three combined peak. The separately reported overlap result resolves that distinction without selecting a favorable domain.
