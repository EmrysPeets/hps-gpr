# Traditional comparison at frozen GP-selected masses

This directory is owned by the independent HEP/statistical reviewer. It implements a bounded comparison on the original released individual spectra at the six frozen GP-selected peaks. All 30 predeclared fits were executed after the saved persistence trigger became true. The final numerical gates pass; this does not qualify every polynomial as an adequate background.

The default command prints the prospective plan and verifies the two sealed input files against their presentation manifest; it does not fit:

```bash
python3 -B study_results/v4p9p16_candidate_removal_20260906/traditional/run_traditional.py
```

`PLAN.json` records all 30 fixed model/window combinations, exact whole-bin windows, resolutions, input hashes and the shifted 21 MeV support window. After the persistence trigger, execution requires both its saved artifact and a human-readable reason:

```bash
python3 -B study_results/v4p9p16_candidate_removal_20260906/traditional/run_traditional.py \
  --execute --trigger-file PATH_TO_SAVED_PERSISTENCE_RESULT \
  --trigger-note 'Measured persistence result authorizing this comparison'
```

The baseline choices and their HPS literature origin are documented in `../review/HEP_INTERVENTION_PROTOCOL_REVIEW.md`. Each baseline has four fixed variants, changing degree by minus/plus one or total width by minus/plus two resolutions, one change at a time. No variant is selected by its p-value.

The fit jointly profiles an exponential-polynomial background and a signed Gaussian yield in the original native-bin Poisson likelihood. The Gaussian mass and resolution are fixed. The reported amplitude is the full untruncated Gaussian yield; a saved window fraction converts it to the fitted-window yield. The expected count in every bin must remain positive. The three fixed free-fit starts must agree. Stationarity, positive local covariance, free/null nesting and higher-quadrature checks are required. Failures are retained and prevent an accepted summary.

`derived/points/` contains the exact observed bins, templates, quadrature arrays, free/null expectations and coefficients, gradients, Hessians, covariance and per-fit metadata. `derived/fit_summary.csv` contains all retained variants with signed roots, nominal local references, deviance/ndof and numerical diagnostics. The local convention is `p0=sf(max(r,0))`, hence 0.5 for a nonpositive root. Deviance goodness-of-fit probabilities are nominal diagnostics. Neither probability is adjusted for GP mass selection, model variants, other analysis choices, or the decision to perform this comparison.

After all variants pass, `make_figures.py` reads those outputs and creates baseline count/residual panels and all-variant root comparisons. It never fits. The residual plot subtracts the background-only fit and displays both the total signal-plus-background change and the isolated signal component; their difference is the profiled polynomial response. The plots are not final significance or coverage results. No random toys, new data or new limits are generated by this implementation.

The first numerical attempt is retained in `qa/initial_attempt/`. A final Newton step in two fixed starts at 2021/65 MeV was below the likelihood roundoff level. The common solver now permits a near-stationary refinement only when it halves the scaled score and preserves NLL within 1e-9. All original final score, covariance, multistart and quadrature gates remain unchanged. The mathematical likelihood and all 30 statistical choices were unchanged. The entire fixed fit set was replayed.

`make_paper_figures.py` creates separate `traditional_YEAR_display.pdf/png` count/residual panels for the portrait analysis note. These paper-sized figures have at least 8-point type, share legends outside the data, and omit the variant row because the note retains the complete 30-fit table. The original three-row supplementary figures remain byte-identical. Rendered PDF and text checks are recorded in `qa/paper_visual_review.json`.
