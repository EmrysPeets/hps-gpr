\begin{abstract}
This note documents the Heavy Photon Search (HPS) Gaussian-process-regression (GPR)
workflow for a narrow prompt
$A^\prime \rightarrow e^+e^-$ resonance in the HPS invariant-mass spectrum. The
analysis performs global background interpolation, local signal extraction with
Gaussian-constrained nuisance parameters for the correlated background uncertainty,
modified-frequentist
\CLs\ ($\mathrm{CL}_{s+b}/\mathrm{CL}_b$) upper limits in signal-yield and
$\eps^2$ space, and a statistically independent multi-dataset combination in a shared
$\eps^2$ parameter of interest. The signal model is implemented as a full-range
Gaussian template whose blinded-bin slice enters the local likelihood without
renormalization, so the fitted amplitude retains the interpretation of a total local
signal yield. The note also summarizes the radiative-fraction normalization,
length-scale parameterization, toy-validation program, and staged path from the
published 2015 and 2016 HPS prompt searches toward a fully unblinded combination.
Version 4 separates the mass interval searched for a signal from the wider spectrum
used to train each GP. Version 4.1 kept those supports and revisited one numerical
range after the v4 observed scan showed that all 142 full-2016 fits saturated the
resolution-scaled length-scale ceiling. A controlled scan of
$k_{\max,2016}=8,10,12,15,$ and 20 identified 12 as the first nonbinding value
followed by a stable plateau. Version 4.2 accepts that factor-12 setting and completes
the 90\% confidence-level combined search using the full 2015 and 2016 samples and
the reviewed 2021 10\% sample. The principal result is the shared-$\eps^2$ observed
asymptotic \CLs{} limit with central expected intervals from exactly 300
background-only joint pseudoexperiments per mass. A common pseudoexperiment index is
shared by every dataset active at that mass. The three constituent distributions
and the three pairwise combinations are additionally documented with auxiliary
100-pseudoexperiment conditional limit bands that reuse the first 100 entries of
the accepted parent stream. Local asymptotic $p_0$ values are shown separately.
The observed 65~MeV local minimum is
documented with an exact fixed-state extraction, sideband context, profiled
residuals, and a comparison of the standalone and shared-coupling estimates.
An August 6 addendum adds an observed-equivalent BaBar comparison, exact
fixed-mass common-binning and physical-profile checks, and two fixed-seed
conditional central-window replacements. These diagnostics do not alter the
accepted v4.2 card or principal result and introduce no projected bands,
coverage statement, or scan-calibrated global claim.
An August 7 appendix defines an operational eight-feature catalogue from the
seven distinct v4.2 limit curves and applies dataset-specific
$\pm2.5\sigma_m$ native-histogram inpainting.  Three joint GP-posterior plus
Poisson conditional replicates, together with a separate deterministic mean
lane, show reciprocal model-mediated cross-response between interventions in
the 65 and 80~MeV regions, which propagates into pairwise and all-three limits.
This response is a property of the shared nonlocal GP/background-profile
refit and is compatible with mediation by the adjacent deficit/background
structure; no physical-dependence or shared-event interpretation is assigned.
A
common-axis 65~MeV positional comparison and a conditional v11
SIMP/TM kinematic atlas are included.  The exact v16 event-level sample used
for the observed-limit histogram is unavailable locally, so the atlas is a
descriptive selection/mechanism comparison.  These
data-selected interventions do not replace the observed limit and the three
replicates are not expected-limit or coverage toys.
An August 10 v4.3 addendum uses a new diagnostic ROOT product whose canonical
2000-, 4000-, and 8000-bin invariant-mass histograms are bitwise identical to
the frozen v4.2 2021 input.  Exhaustive topology and stored-trigger partitions
close bin-by-bin, and four mass-versus-control surfaces permit a deterministic
selected-row morphology study in the exact v16 production.  A jointly masked,
sideband-trained conditional-composition GPR is checked with target-blind fake
gaps, four mass binnings, four continuous-control groupings, and single-target
mask variants.  It uses zero pseudoexperiments and computes no category limit,
candidate yield, or significance.  The saved variables show sub-percent
conditional-composition differences whose direction and stability depend on
the variable and deterministic analysis choice.  Joint masking of the 65 and
80~MeV regions protects the interpolation from shared GP/background-profile
cross-response.  The cross-response describes the common fitting procedure,
not a physical relation between the two local mass features.
This diagnostic does not alter the accepted v4.2 card, observed limit, bands,
or candidate catalogue.
An August 11 extension compares normalized central-window and adjacent-
sideband shapes for momentum sum, vertex-projection significance, minimum
track $|y_0|$, electron 3D-hit count, topology, and stored-trigger category at
51, 65, 80, 90, 117, and 162~MeV.  Region totals use fractional overlap of
the 0.125-MeV mass-bin edges with the fixed resolution-scaled windows at each
anchor.  These histogram-level comparisons add neither a signal yield nor a
significance or limit result.  The largest central-to-sideband-chord shape
distances occur at the 51~MeV lower-boundary region and, among the interior
anchors, at 65~MeV; the corresponding 80~MeV differences are smaller.
The v4.1 and v4.5 ten-toy source/exposure studies are retained in an appendix.
Version 4.6 uniformly reruns all 100 background pseudoexperiments for the
$1\%\times10$, native 10\%, $1\%\times100$, and $10\%\times10$ ensembles at
65, 90, 120, 180, and 210~MeV with 0-, 1-, 3-, and 5-$\sigma_A$ injections.
It uses the frozen v4.2 2021 card, including $k_{\max}=15$ and the
$2.25\sigma_m$ blind/training geometry, and a predeclared adaptive repeat gate
whose branch choice uses only GP log marginal likelihood and reproducibility.
The gate excludes six irreproducible state keys from 8000 while retaining every
statistical extreme that passes the pull-blind technical gate; all 80 closure cells contain
99 or 100 accepted toys.  The pilot-selected $1\%\times10$, 65~MeV
zero-signal offset is confirmed in the independent 90-toy reserve:
$\bar p=0.768$ with 95\% interval $[0.570,0.965]$.  The full-100 value is
$0.789$, and the analytic stress-truth mean gives 0.837, identifying a local
functional-form/GP closure offset rather than a few bad toys.  Stable
factor-15 contacts remain in 4.25\% of accepted $1\%\times100$ fits and 6.72\%
of accepted $10\%\times10$ fits.  These are conditional stress-test results,
not direct \CLs{} coverage, expected-limit bands, a scan-wise significance
calibration, or a change to the accepted v4.2 observed result.
Version 4.7 responds to the near-threshold closure pattern with independent
source fits over 35--80~MeV.  A logistic turn-on times an exponentiated
degree-five Chebyshev series is the lowest common candidate that passes the
fixed native-bin and five-bin goodness-of-fit gates for the 2021 1\% and 10\%
sources.  Twenty Poisson background spectra per source-derived scenario are
then tested at 55, 60, 65, and 70~MeV with 0-, 1-, 3-, and
5-$\sigma_A$ injections under the unchanged v4.2/v4.5 analysis semantics.
The dedicated truth reduces the $1\%\times10$ 65~MeV mean pull from the
archived, unpaired v4.6 value 0.789 to 0.300, but native 10\% remains at
0.641 and the two scenarios show additional near-threshold offsets at 55 and
70~MeV.  Thus the study is partial mitigation rather than uniform closure.
All 640 extraction states are retained after a pull-blind optimizer gate,
amended following an initial numerical-ledger audit and applied in a uniform
rerun.  The 20-toy cells are correlated conditional diagnostics, not coverage,
expected bands, observed limits, or evidence that the observed 65~MeV feature
has a particular origin.  No accepted card or v4.2 observed result changes.
Version 4.8 tests two residual-structured 2021 generating means: a source-only
selected fixed-knot log spline and a fixed, smoothly overlapping three-region
blend.  Both fail the frozen support-wide source-qualification and
signal-influence gates, so neither is promoted or described as unable to learn
a narrow signal.  Exactly 20 backgrounds are nevertheless completed as a
requested conditional extraction stress for native 1\%, $1\%\times10$,
$1\%\times100$, native 10\%, and $10\%\times10$ at 65, 90, 120, 180, and
210~MeV with 0-, 1-, 3-, and 5-$\sigma_A$ injections: 2000 accepted states per
model and no exclusions.  The K2 form is less pathological than the regional
blend, with three versus sixteen of 25 exploratory material zero-signal flags,
but its native-1\% factor-25 closure is upper-bound-censored in 345 of 400
states.  A post-closure, pull-blind study with eight fresh backgrounds
qualifies factor 50 against a factor-75 sentinel for that lane only; it does
not rerun or uncensor the closure, qualify a common all-lane ceiling, or alter
the production v4.2 factor-15 card.  These results remain conditional stress
diagnostics rather than coverage, expected bands, observed-data bias, limits,
or exclusions.
\end{abstract}
