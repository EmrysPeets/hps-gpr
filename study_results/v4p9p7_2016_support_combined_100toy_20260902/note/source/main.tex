\begin{abstract}
This note describes a Gaussian-process search for narrow prompt $e^+e^-$ resonances
in HPS data.  Version 4.9.7 adds a source-conditioned test of the lower GP-support
edge for full-2016 inference and a post-unblinding audit of the earlier 2021 65~MeV
feature.  The accepted v4.2 combination and the v4.9.5 2021-only scan remain distinct
historical result states.

The 2016 study uses the pre-existing 10\% development sample/subset for partial
observed-shape information.  Before the support decision, the full-2016 array enters
only through one scalar normalization over 26--210~MeV; no support-specific full-data
fitted amplitude, local $p_0$, or upper limit enters selection.  The hybrid generating
mean also contains an explicitly waived broad-tail component whose archived ROOT fit
record has \texttt{fit\_ok=false}.  It is used only as a conditional stress truth, not
as a qualified physical background truth or coverage generator.

Phase~1 tests lower edges from 28 through 34~MeV at 44, 49, 54, and 59~MeV using 25
paired backgrounds and matched 0-, 2-, and 5-$\sigma_A$ injections.  Every edge has
only 3 of 12 cell means below 0.75 in magnitude and only 1 of 4 background-only means
below 0.75; every edge violates the predeclared 1.25 gross-bias guard.  A uniform
analytic-mean diagnostic shows the same alternating mass-dependent mismatch without
Poisson fluctuations.  The frozen rule therefore returns no provisional edge and
requires the study to stop without retuning.

Consequently no 75-background continuation, support-specific full-2016 observed
scan, updated combined upper limit on $\eps^2$, or 100-toy combined band is produced.
Their absence is an enforced analysis decision, not a zero result.  Separately, the
unchanged 2021 counts at 65~MeV give local asymptotic $Z=4.252$ with the v4.2 support
and 2.403 with the v4.9.5 support.  The support-dependent GP state assigns more
continuum to the masked region and leaves a smaller narrow-signal coefficient.  The
defensible conclusion is that the narrow-signal attribution is not robust to the GP
support prescription, not that the feature has been proved background or signal has
been excluded.
\end{abstract}
