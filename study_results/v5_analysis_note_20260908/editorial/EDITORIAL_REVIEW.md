## Important interpretation decisions

- The original figure 9 injection strength is 2 or 5 times a **fixed profiled Fisher reference error**, with 500 spectra in each mass/strength/background cell. Both methods receive the same physical signal. These are not guaranteed measured significances.
- At 71 MeV, the five-reference-error mean pulls are −2.33 fixed and −0.81 profiled, but true-yield exclusion rates are 349/500 and 145/500. The improvement in mean pull motivates profiling while the exclusion failure remains explicit.
- The calibration completed 456 coordinates; numerical precision, conditional coverage screens and physical background qualification are separate statements. All 41 common-range combined endpoints resolve for both methods. The later 232-mass union was not calibrated by this study.
- No adjusted calibration flags do not establish percent-level coverage. The 500-toy cell size and multiplicity-screen thresholds are explained.
- The 2016 stress background is a development-sample threshold construction joined to an archived continuation with a retained failed fit-convergence/status flag. It is not independently established as the true full-data background. The 2021 anchored residual construction also has a poor broad-tail fit metric.
- The large stress-reference score at combined 76 MeV accompanies observed raw signed local significance +0.166 and asymptotic local p=0.434. A Gaussian score near 9 is not a 9-sigma particle result. The 0/200,000 and 0/1,000 exceedance results retain their separate one-sided 95% sampling bounds.
- The significance-GP covariance is estimated from coherent full spectra and one-bin responses. Historical independently generated mass-local ensembles cannot supply that covariance by joining toy IDs.
- Historical support qualification used an amended practical criterion and retains the 55 MeV exceptions. Its final-data-unblinding readiness sentence must not survive as a current conclusion. The root and audit agent have exact baseline replacement locations.
