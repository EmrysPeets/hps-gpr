\section{Revision scope and reproducibility}
This v5.8.5.3 revision adds plot and interpretation changes only. It uses 1,310 saved scope--mass coordinates, with 163, 283, 401 and 463 points for 2015, 2016, 2021 and the shared-coupling combination. All requested observed local curves are calculated directly from their saved signed roots; the generating-source offset and response width never enter that calculation. There are no new likelihood fits, GP optimizations, Poisson experiments or Gaussian-field draws in this revision.

The local and global constructions are kept separate in the numerical ledger. The local columns contain the conventional unshifted asymptotic display. The global columns use the saved 200,000 Gaussian raw maxima per scope, checked with 256 complete Poisson scans, and remain conditional on the existing nominal GP sources. The expanded Figure 4 panels use those same complete Poisson scans. No reference-standardized maximum ensemble is substituted into the raw global calculation.

The earlier consolidated report's statistical, source-transfer and injection studies are retained as prior results. Their 603 deterministic source states, 768 Poisson fits and 500,000-field regional comparison were completed in that earlier review; they were not rerun for this revision. All prior source-independence, selection, exposure, overlap and rare-tail qualifications continue to apply.

The source bundle contains the revised LaTeX, all included figures, the four exact saved field files, new plotting and verification scripts, per-mass raw p-value/significance and toy-moment tables, and the prior report and result ledgers. It reproduces the requested new figures and the revised report without the live parent checkout. The earlier numerical studies are retained through their saved figures/results; their full refit environments remain in their original packages.

A SHA-256 manifest pins 331 original files in the previous consolidated study; a final comparison checks that they are unchanged. Validation checks the unshifted transformations, mass coordinates, raw-maximum tail counts, table values and report text, and includes rendered-page inspection and an isolated PDF rebuild. Rebuild commands are in \texttt{README.md}. No previous release is overwritten.
