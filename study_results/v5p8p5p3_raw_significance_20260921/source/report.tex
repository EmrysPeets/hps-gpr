\noindent This v5.8.5.3 revision adds unshifted local p-value and significance curves for 2015, 2016, 2021 and the shared-coupling combination, separate global curves for the raw maximum, and source-response diagnostics for all four scopes. Earlier reference-local curves did use centering and scaling in every scope; the new local curves do not. The moving blind half-width remains $2.25\sigma_m$.
\fig{significance_schematic}{The two Gaussian processes and the choice of statistical ordering. The background GP is part of the fitted analysis. The significance-field GP approximates the distribution of that analysis under a specified source. It need not be independent of the background model. Independent evidence is needed to qualify the source and to validate the approximation. Raw and reference-standardized maxima are different tests, even with identical observed events and likelihood fits.}
