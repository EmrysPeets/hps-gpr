# Hit-selection provenance for v5.7.1

`selection_criteria.json` records the adopted per-run geometric hit criteria, their sources, the 2015 internal-note versus publication distinction, and the current user correction for 2021. The two copied v5.0.4 TeX files are unmodified historical evidence; their older 2021 statements are superseded for this study. They are not current selection instructions.

Adopted criteria: 2015 electron and positron each need at least five paired 3D stations, with positron L1 and L2 mandatory; 2016 both need at least five paired 3D stations, with no extra mandatory inner layer; 2021 positron needs at least ten individual 2D sensor-plane hits and electron at least eight, with no added mandatory inner stations. All comparators are inclusive.

The 2015 internal PDF is identified by absolute source path, SHA-256 hash, pages and excerpts in the ledger; it is not copied. The 2016 tracking minimum comes from the published analysis, Section III.2.2. Exact event-cut equivalence to the selected mass histograms remains unverified.
