# Independent review of the adopted hit criteria

The reviewed `hit_features`, `features_from_groups`, `selection_mask` and projected `vertical_edges` implement the criteria in `inputs/selection/selection_criteria.json`. No actionable bug was found. The first boosted daughter is explicitly designated the positron; theta-star and phi-star refer to that daughter. The second daughter is the electron. Charge assignment matters for the asymmetric 2021 thresholds.

Individual views are unioned over sensor alternatives before counting, so overlapping hole/slot alternatives do not inflate the number of hits. Complete 3D stations require both axial and stereo views in the same detector half. The 2021 selected condition uses individual views directly (positron >=10, electron >=8), without requiring paired stations. The 2015 and 2016 conditions use >=5 complete stations per daughter. Only the 2015 positron has mandatory stations 1 and 2, matching the adopted internal-note convention. The strict reference requires every paired station on both daughters and is a subset of each selected condition.

`vertical_edges` projects all sensor faces onto beam-distance versus vertical position, unions alternative projected faces, applies the same hit criteria and permits either positron half. As all sensor vertices have positive projected beam distance, the interval between minimum and maximum corner slopes is the projected angular interval of each convex face. This is a projected envelope, not full 3D ray acceptance: horizontal seams are intentionally discarded. The displayed mass scales must retain that qualification.

Meaningful validation is reproduced by `scripts/validate_hit_selection.py`, with output in `qa/hit_selection_validation.json`. Actual finite-geometry intersections agree exactly with an independent implementation transforming the complete ray into each sensor's local coordinates. Only 1024 directions at one mass for each old geometry and 4096 directions at one 2021 mass were used. Strict all-station events are a subset of selected events, and strict projected intervals are contained in the selected projected intervals. Targeted criterion checks demonstrate that missing electron L1 is allowed under the adopted 2015 convention when five paired stations remain; transferring that absence to the positron fails. Neither charge has an added inner-station requirement in 2016.

A concrete zero-field 2021 example has parent mass 205.7 MeV, energy 3740 MeV, positron cos(theta-star)=0.75 and phi-star=7*pi/4. It intersects 10 positron sensor views but only three complete positron stations (1,2,4); the electron intersects 8 views forming four complete stations (1,2,3,4). It passes the adopted 10/8 selection, fails the all-station reference, and fails when the charges are swapped. Thus replacing individual 10/8 hits with five/four paired stations would incorrectly reject an actual geometric configuration.

These checks concern the geometric hit logic only. They do not establish reconstruction or selected-histogram cut equivalence and do not yet include magnetic transport.
