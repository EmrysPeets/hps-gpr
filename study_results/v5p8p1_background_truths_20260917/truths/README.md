# Coherent candidate backgrounds

`backgrounds.npz` contains five positive 720-bin expected-count spectra plus `edges_GeV` and the common `observed` source. These arrays were frozen before the root agent evaluated them, and their SHA-256 remains `9f71ae6f59729478443bdfd6abd0a9837323eeaebacce541b3c4fd91d1ed7263`. `supplementary.npz` contains the sixth candidate `gp_blocked_local` with the same edges and source counts. Every candidate is one complete spectrum used unchanged at every tested signal mass. No construction chooses a background using favorable observed roots or limits.

The first five are: the archived **conditional hybrid stress histogram**, whose broad component retains a failed source-fit flag; a full-data GP mean with the frozen 76 MeV kernel; the same GP with half the length scale; an excluded-block GP with that common kernel; and separately fitted rising/falling log-cubic models joined across 50–75 MeV. GP transformations, kernels and block geometry are recorded in `protocol.json` and `block_geometry.npz`. The regional fits are unconstrained Poisson regressions, not monotonic regressions. Their realized curve has one turnover, but it inadequately describes the threshold shape.

The blocked construction uses fixed centers spaced by 5 MeV and compact positive C2 blending weights extending 5 MeV from each center. Each component excludes a region broad enough to keep all positively weighted query bins at least the extraction-window half-width from training counts over 39–180 MeV. There is no total-count renormalization, which would reintroduce excluded source counts. Each final truth is the fixed weighted combination of the component means, not a background switched according to the eventual tested signal mass.

The original blocked curve is a failed control: its common kernel and one-sided edge extrapolation produce seven slope-sign changes, a total 9.35% above the observed count total, and a mean Pearson discrepancy of about 5029 per bin. Smooth partition weights ensure differentiability; they do not guarantee a suitable source model. The regional cubic curve likewise remains a failed comparison (about 349 per bin), with its worst mismatch near the threshold.

The sixth candidate uses each block center's reviewed kernel and uses the full-data nominal GP mean for blocks without both sidebands. The same smooth weights blend these explicitly self-fit edge components. This was a construction-based amendment, not selection on local significance. It reduces the mean Pearson discrepancy to about 113 per bin but remains source-inadequate. Its self-fit fallback weight is 1 at 39 and 42 MeV, approximately zero at 50 MeV, and zero at 66, 76, 90, 92, 117 and 180 MeV. Therefore its low-mass edge is not an independent blocked prediction. `supplementary_manifest.json` stores the fallback weight for every bin.

These source residual summaries are descriptive; no formal chi-squared goodness-of-fit probability is claimed for means learned from the same data. The nearly unit residual summaries of the full-data GP candidates do not qualify them as independent null backgrounds.

## Source-signal absorption test

`source_absorption.py` adds a deterministic signal to the **observed source before rebuilding** each candidate. This differs from injecting a signal into an already-frozen truth and then testing the extraction. The two fixed source injections are at 76 and 90 MeV with nominal amplitude five times the original linear uncertainty. The inherited full-support Gaussian template retains its tails outside the extraction mask; the actual injected yield inside the mask is recorded separately.

The nominal full-data GP absorbs approximately 88.7% and 88.4% of the injected within-window yield into the reconstructed background; its projection along the signal template is approximately 71.9% and 72.0%. Halving the length scale increases these to about 103.6%/103.5% for the window integral and 94.5%/94.6% for the template projection. Fractions can exceed one because background changes are not a probability or an efficiency.

The local-kernel blocked construction absorbs only about 2.1% and 10.0% of the source increment in the corresponding windows, but it produces substantial changes elsewhere in the spectrum. Its full-support change divided by injected yield is approximately +5.79 and -6.13. Thus reduced local absorption does not establish a stable coherent global null. All regenerated spectra are preserved in `source_absorption.npz`; no truth was replaced or selected using this test.

`manifest.json`, `supplementary_manifest.json`, `shape_diagnostics.csv`, `source_absorption_protocol.json` and `builder_validation.json` retain definitions, hashes and checks. The main construction entry point is `../scripts/build_truths.py`; the supplementary constructor is its `construct_local_blocked` function. The source-absorption runner is `source_absorption.py`. Recomputing these constructors requires the frozen runtime and source inputs identified in the manifests. Saved expected-count arrays themselves are independent of that runtime.
