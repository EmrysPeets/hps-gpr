# Statistical review of v5.8.1

Review complete. The study answers a conditional model-robustness question; it does not certify a new local particle significance. All852 deterministic mass/truth coordinates and72 fixed-yield injection scenarios were evaluated. Of36 requested local null scenarios,33 completed with512 experiments each, giving16,896 accepted null fits; three failed scenarios have no reported probability. The numerical failures remain visible rather than being removed from a tail ensemble or accepted with a looser tolerance.

Independent saved-array checks reproduce the truth identities, native edges, positive means, uniform142-mass scan, every deterministic summary and injection recovery fraction, unchanged observed roots, local exceedance counts, exact binomial interval inversion, unique seed streams, and source-injection projections. Original observed roots reproduce exactly. The detailed deterministic review remains `passed:false` because the gp_blocked fit at45 MeV has score5.43e-7, above the declared2e-7 gate. Execution is complete, but numerical qualification is not universal. The three local failures are gp_blocked_local at42 MeV and gp_blocked at66 and76 MeV. The independent local arithmetic review passes for the33 completed scenarios; it does not convert failed scenarios into qualified results.

The six coherent background constructions have stress-root RMS values4.9674 (archived),0.6093 (full GP, nominal kernel),1.7004 (full GP, half length scale),102.6726 (original blocked GP),13.9285 (regional rise/fall), and53.8721 (supplementary blocked GP). Each is a single complete positive vector reused across masses. The nominal full GP therefore substantially reduces the archived shape mismatch under the fixed extraction. This result is comparative, not an unbiased-null certification: its maximum absolute offset is1.392, and its nominal5% raw local test rejects101/512 experiments at90 MeV and97/512 at117 MeV, about19.7% and18.9% respectively.

The blocked and simple regional curves are not credible replacements on these tests. Original blocked source Pearson residual per bin is5028.9 and its total expectation is9.35% above the observed total. The supplementary version improves these measures but still has Pearson residual per bin113.5 and severe moving-fit offsets. The regional construction has Pearson residual per bin349.4 despite an analytically C2 join. These are descriptive source-discrepancy measures, not calibrated chi-square probabilities. Smoothness and positivity alone do not establish interpolation adequacy. The primary block weights sum to one and the exclusion geometry checks pass; its poor performance is substantive interpolation behavior rather than a failure to exclude the specified bins. The supplementary low and high edges use full-data GP fallback and are explicitly not held out, including the42 MeV anchor.

The source-injection experiment makes the circularity concern quantitative. Rebuilding the background after injecting the source with a fixed five-reference-error signal at76 and90 MeV moves88.7% and88.4% of the injected window yield into the nominal full-GP background prediction. The corresponding unweighted L2 template projections are71.9% and72.0% (the report uses the separately verified Poisson-weighted projections71.8% and71.7%). For the half-length-scale source GP the window ratios are103.6% and103.5%, while template projections are94.5% and94.6%. A window-sum response ratio can exceed one and is not an efficiency. These source-construction effects are different from the95.6–99.0% yield recovery observed when injecting a signal into an already frozen truth and then rerunning extraction. Good extraction recovery does not undo signal already absorbed when that truth was built from the data.

The local results consequently depend strongly on the named background. At90,92,117 MeV the nominal full-GP add-one probabilities are0.00585,0.00390,0.00585; the half-length-scale source GP gives0.35867,0.14815,0.46199. The unchanged observed roots are3.42475,3.11542,3.27861. For the first nominal-GP value the95% exact interval is0.00047–0.01404: the simulations do not resolve a precise extreme tail. At nonpositive observed root, the inclusiveq0 tail and interval are exactly1. Zero exceedances among512 experiments imply only the one-sided95% bound p<0.00584; the add-one floor is not a measured Gaussian significance.

All background constructions use the observed source, including the blocked variants through their remaining data, inherited kernels and declared blending. No constructed curve is an independent background measurement. The sixth candidate is an explicitly recorded supplementary construction following a source-interpolation diagnosis; it does not replace the original failed control. Choosing among these curves from observed local p-values, or silently dropping a numerically failed member from an all-candidate envelope, would change the statistical procedure. An envelope may only name the successfully computed members to which it applies; an all-six envelope is unavailable at anchors with failed members.

The defensible conclusion is that the extreme archived response is strongly truth-dependent, and a coherent nominal-GP reference provides much better internal extraction closure. The additional source-injection test shows why that improvement is insufficient to qualify it as a physical null. A new release would require an independently justified source/background family, a fixed selection rule, signal-contamination controls, and repeated-experiment local calibration before any coherent scan-global calculation.
