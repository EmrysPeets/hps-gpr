\textbf{Note:} Global significances are not reported in this selection of HPS
internal documentation. Every quoted $p_0$ or $Z$ is a local, fixed-mass asymptotic
diagnostic.

\medskip
\textbf{Abstract.} This excerpt follows the HPS search for a
dark photon, $A^\prime$, from the detector and $e^+e^-$ spectra through a
Gaussian-process description of the smooth background and the validation of a narrow
resonance search. Four pseudoexperiment ensembles probe the extraction at different
statistical scales, with matched zero-, one-, three-, and five-reference-sigma signal
tests. Each scenario passes the documented validation checks, and the injected-signal
response remains stable at 96.6--96.9\%. That agreement across sample sizes is the
basis for saying that the analysis is ready for the planned full-data unblinding. The
current observed results use full 2015, full 2016, and the available 2021 10\% sample
in three standalone and four shared-coupling fits. The smallest all-three local value
occurs at 66~MeV, with $p_0=2.845\times10^{-3}$ and $Z=2.765$; it is a local
fluctuation, not a discovery claim.
