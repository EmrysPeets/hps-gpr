\begin{abstract}
This note describes a Gaussian-process search for narrow prompt $e^+e^-$ resonances
in HPS data.  Version 4.9.6 is an editorial revision of the validated v4.9.5 analysis:
the data, analysis cards, fits, figures, and numerical results are unchanged.  The main
text has been reorganized so that the detector inputs lead directly to the statistical
method, its validation, and the observed result.

The latest validation varies only the lower edge of the GP support for the native 2021
10\% sample.  The search remains fixed at 50--250~MeV, and the same 100
source-conditioned backgrounds are tested at 55, 60, 65, and 70~MeV with matched
    0-, 2-, and 5-$\sigma_A$ injections.  No candidate survives the original rejection
    rule: each has at least one injection cell with $|\overline p|\geq0.5$ whose two-sided
    90\% interval excludes zero.  A broader practical rule, fixed after the
first 25 backgrounds but before the independent continuation, selects 36~MeV from the
eligible candidates.  The remaining 75 backgrounds confirm the 36--300~MeV support
without further tuning.

In the complete ensemble, 9 of 12 injection-cell means and 3 of 4 background-only
means meet the adopted $|\overline p|<0.75$ condition.  The three exceptions all occur
at 55~MeV, with means $-0.773$, $-0.841$, and $-0.960$ for the 0-, 2-, and
5-$\sigma_A$ injections.  The other nine means range from $-0.110$ to $+0.325$.
These source-conditioned injection--extraction tests do not measure direct \CLs{}
coverage or prove that the background model is unbiased for every smooth spectrum.

After fixing the support, we scan the native 2021 10\% data with the profiled
asymptotic $\widetilde q_\mu$ statistic.  The result comprises 201 observed 90\%
\CLs{} limits on signal yield and $\eps^2$, together with local analytic $p_0$ values.
No expected-limit bands or \CLs{} toys are used.  The smallest local value is
$p_0=0.00247$ at 78~MeV ($Z=2.81$); without a look-elsewhere calibration, it is not a
global significance.
\end{abstract}
