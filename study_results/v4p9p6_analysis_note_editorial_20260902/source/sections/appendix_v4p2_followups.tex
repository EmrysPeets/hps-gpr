\subsection{Common-binning and profiled 65 MeV extraction}
\label{app:v4p2-common-binning}

The fixed-mass follow-up documented that exact 0.625~MeV common bins could not be
formed from the histogram-only 2015 and 2016 inputs.  A source-compatible 0.5~MeV
refit and a broader 1.25~MeV stress test preserved the qualitative 65~MeV extraction.
The physical-domain profile display also separated the nonnegative interval used for
limits from the signed Wald coordinate used for local extraction.  These were
observed-data robustness checks, not a choice made from toy coverage.

\subsection{Conditional central-window replacement provenance}
\label{app:v4p2-pseudo65}

Two fixed-seed pseudo-observations replaced the 2021 central region and reran the
complete moving-mask analysis.  A later deterministic functional-mean lane separated
a coherent source-truth/GP shoulder below 65~MeV from the additional Poisson
fluctuation of one draw.  Ten fixed-GP-mean replacement draws compared the discretely
equivalent $2.25$/$2.5\sigma_m$ scope with a $3\sigma_m$ scope.  Those finite draws
were correlated, conditional interventions.  They did not refit a source generator,
establish direct \CLs{} coverage, or measure the probability that the observed
65~MeV feature is background.

For v4.9, the relevant lesson is limited: replacing a central window can reveal
analysis-mediated response, but it is not a substitute for source-qualified
full-refit functional-form toys.  The threshold-support decision is therefore based on
the matched-reference study in Section~\ref{sec:toys}, with the older window material
retained only for provenance.
