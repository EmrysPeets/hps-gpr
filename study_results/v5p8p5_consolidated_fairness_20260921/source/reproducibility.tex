\section{Scope, provenance and reproducibility}
The numerical claims are separated in the package into archived results and new diagnostics. The new work consists of the retained-raw-ordering audit from saved ensembles; the ten-percent-correlation and regional-maximum study; and a bounded 2021 source-transfer and injection study. The archived v5.8.0--v5.8.5 sources, fields, ledgers and important plots were read directly. The referenced v5.0.5 sections and arXiv paper were checked against their actual text.

The response-field comparison uses the saved 200,000 Gaussian maxima and 256 complete Poisson scans per scope. The regional diagnostic adds 500,000 correlated Gaussian fields for 2016 and compares the same covariance, threshold and domain. Its Monte Carlo interval is distinct from that of the saved ensemble. The new 2021 study uses 603 deterministic mass/source states, 64 coherent six-anchor Poisson experiments for each of two sources, and two source-rebuilding injection anchors. These are local diagnostics, not a new full-domain Poisson calibration.

The source bundle contains the LaTeX report, vector figures, the new scripts and ledgers, the precise high-$p_{\rm sum}$ input histogram, copied engine dependencies, and the frozen fields used in the statistical audit. Commands in \texttt{README.md} rebuild the report and the new calculations separately. The frozen files needed to render the report are included; historical studies are represented by their saved outputs and code snapshots, rather than a promise that every historical fit can be rerun from this smaller bundle.

A SHA-256 parent manifest records 3,782 original files. The closing audit checks that these released files are unchanged. Report verification includes numerical assertions, PDF text extraction, rendered-page inspection and an isolated rebuild. The work was constrained to one hour and at most 20 additional percentage points of account-wide weekly usage; the measured completion time and usage snapshots are recorded in the delivery metadata.
