before completion when an exact rebinned-bin audit showed that the 39 MeV
search endpoint had zero low-side training-bin centers: the lower blind edge
was 35.063 MeV, while the first rebin-5 center was 35.125 MeV.

No row from this directory is used in the v4 observed scan, optimizer review,
expected bands, p-value diagnostics, figures, or note. The production v4 card
moves only the 2016 lower fit-support endpoint to 30 MeV and is rerun from
scratch.
