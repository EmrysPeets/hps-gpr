\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This draft organizes the HPS prompt dark-photon GPR analysis into a PRD-scale
manuscript. The analysis searches for a narrow prompt $\Ap\to e^+e^-$ resonance in
selected HPS invariant-mass spectra, models the smooth QED background with
sideband-trained Gaussian process regression, propagates the GPR covariance through a
profile likelihood, and sets upper limits on $\eps^2$ using the modified-frequentist
$\CLs$ construction. Statistically independent HPS samples are combined with a common
$\eps^2$ parameter of interest and independent data-set-specific nuisance parameters.

The current staged result combines the fully unblinded 2015 sample with 2016 10\% and
2021 1\% validation samples. It is a coherent review baseline and a strong template
for the final journal article, but it is not yet the final full-statistics exclusion.
The publication-critical remaining steps are bounded: freeze the final 2016 and 2021
inputs, finalize the radiative-fraction and systematic-penalty model, regenerate the
observed and expected limit figures from the production outputs, and replace
effective-trials discovery overlays with a scan-level toy calibration of the global
significance.

Once those pieces are complete, the GPR framework documented here supports a compact
HPS prompt-resonance publication: a data-driven smooth-background interpolation, a
likelihood-level shared-coupling combination, and a transparent comparison with the
published HPS prompt searches and external visible-dark-photon limits.
