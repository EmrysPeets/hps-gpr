\subsection{Residual-structured 2021 generating-mean stress tests}
\label{sec:v4p8p3-residual-functional-forms}

\begin{center}
\fbox{%
\begin{minipage}{0.94\linewidth}
\textbf{FAILED QUALIFICATION --- REQUESTED CONDITIONAL STRESS ONLY.}
Both residual-structured means fail the frozen source-qualification and
signal-influence gates.  The two 20-background extraction ensembles are
complete, but completion does not rescue either failure.  The fixed-knot
native-1\% lane is additionally upper-bound-censored at the pilot's factor-25
fallback.  Nothing in this subsection is physical background truth,
observed-data bias, coverage, an expected band, a limit, an exclusion, or
authority to change the production card.
\end{minipage}}
\end{center}

\subsubsection{Post-exploratory construction and frozen claim boundary}

The residual patterns exposed by the preceding v4.8 study motivated two
alternative generating means.  This is therefore post-exploratory model
development, not a prospectively blind choice.  The model structures and the
source-only knot-selection rule were frozen before the signal-influence audit
and before any GPR extraction result from this study was inspected.  No pull,
recovery, local significance, confidence limit, or length-scale response enters
the model choice.

The first candidate is a natural spline in log yield relative to the frozen
v4.8 rigid threshold mean.  The two-knot candidate has fixed interior nodes at
105 and 180~MeV, while the three-knot comparator has fixed nodes at 90, 160,
and 220~MeV.  Five-fold blocked source-only Poisson deviance and a
one-standard-error rule select between them.  Knot locations are never fitted.
For native 10\%, the selected native-1\% shape is transferred with a free
normalization and only the frozen, ridge-penalized, zero-mean contrast allowed
by the protocol.

The second candidate is a three-region geometric blend in log yield.  A
thresholded generalized-gamma low component, positive quadratic
log-Chebyshev middle component, and monotone power-exponential high component
are joined with fixed quintic tapers over 85--125 and 165--215~MeV.  The
boundaries and tapers are fixed.  Native 10\% again receives only the declared
normalization and two bounded regional contrasts; the component shapes are not
refitted.

\subsubsection{Source-only model selection and qualification}

Five blocked source-only folds select the two-knot candidate: its mean
held-out deviance per bin is 1.153, compared with 1.358 for the three-knot
candidate, and all candidate-fold best basins repeat 22--24 times.  This
discrete choice does not make the selected family a qualified source mean.
Table~\ref{tab:v4p8p3-source-qualification} gives the final support-wide
40--300~MeV metrics.  A favorable in-sample value cannot override a failed
blocked-prediction, optimizer-reproducibility, or parameter-bound gate.

\subsubsection{Signal-influence and feature-scale audits}

Signal rigidity is tested rather than inferred from the small number of model
coordinates.  The frozen audit reports the projection of leave-window-out
source refits onto the bin-integrated signal template, the linearized tangent
absorption, deterministic source refits after one-, three-, and five-sigma
injections, and the separation of adjacent extrema in the fitted log-mean
correction.  Optimizer success, constraint feasibility, and repeated recovery
of the best basin are part of the audit.  Passing this audit does not rescue a
failed source-qualification gate.

The predeclared maxima are 0.20$\sigma_A$ for a fake-gap shift, 0.04 for the
conservative tangent fraction, and 0.20 for
$z\lvert f_{\mathrm{abs}}\rvert$.  Both models fail all three.  In particular,
the K2 basis projects as much as 24.5\% of a narrow template in the
linearized metric, while the regional basis reaches 63.1\%.  A small number of
fixed coordinates therefore does \emph{not} establish that the form cannot
learn signal.

\subsubsection{Five-lane nested pseudoexperiment design}

The five reported lanes are explicit in Table~\ref{tab:v4p8p3-five-lane-inventory}.
Within each source family, higher exposures are formed with independent Poisson
increments, so the lanes are nested rather than independent.  Masses and
injection strengths reuse each background realization.  The two source
families are distinct, and the protocol also makes the two model streams
distinct.  Consequently, side-by-side model differences are unpaired and do
not isolate a causal response to functional-form replacement.

\subsubsection{Pull-blind length-ceiling pilot}

Three independent pilot backgrounds per model and source family are kept
disjoint from the 20 closure backgrounds.  The background-only pilot compares
upper factors 15, 20, and 25 at 65, 120, and 210~MeV on identical inputs.  The
common closure ceiling is selected for all models and lanes using only bound
occupancy, same-input nested likelihood, optimizer reproducibility, and the
20-to-25 coordinate plateau.  Pulls, fitted amplitudes, recovery, local
significance, and limits are forbidden inputs.  The production v4.2 factor-15
card remains unchanged regardless of the pilot outcome.

Factor 20 fails: seven states contact its exact/near upper bound, the maximum
$\lvert\Delta\mathrm{LML}\rvert/n_{\rm train}$ from 20 to 25 is 0.01535
against 0.001, and the 95th percentile of
$\lvert\Delta\ell\rvert/\sigma_x$ is 5.0 against 0.03.  The protocol therefore
uses its factor-25 fallback.  Table~\ref{tab:v4p8p3-length-pilot} exposes a
defect in that fallback rule: all nine independent pilot states for the K2 native-1\%
lane also lie exactly at factor 25.  The fallback is not a demonstrated
plateau.

\subsubsection{Conditional zero-signal closure and injected recovery}

Zero injection is reported first.  Every accepted signed pull, the raw-first
optimizer state, any exclusion, the median, and the declared finite-sample mean
and width intervals remain visible.  Nonzero results then report signed pull,
pull width, change in fitted significance, and amplitude recovery for the one-,
three-, and five-sigma injections.  The four strengths and five masses share
backgrounds and must not be presented as independent tests.

Both model collections contain exactly 2000 raw and 2000 accepted rows, 100

The exploratory Holm-plus-$|\bar p|\geq0.2$ screen flags 3 of 25 K2
zero-signal cells, all at 65~MeV, and 16 of 25 regional cells.  The K2 median
absolute zero-signal mean is 0.193 and its maximum is 1.659.  The regional

165--215~MeV middle/high overlap.  This pattern is a conditional model
mismatch, not evidence for a physical feature.

The raw ratio $\widehat A/A_{\rm inj}$ is strongly confounded by these
zero-signal offsets.  Across all 1500 injected rows per model, the paired
response has median $0.9804$ with central 68\% range 0.9678--0.9949 for K2,

tails, together with the independent source-refit audit, forbid a claim of

\subsubsection{Disposition}

Neither residual-structured form is promoted.  K2 is the less pathological
conditional stress truth: it has three exploratory material zero-bias flags,
localized at 65~MeV, compared with sixteen and a strong overlap-aligned pattern
for the regional blend.  Nevertheless K2 fails source qualification and all
signal-influence gates, has two missing native-10\% constrained influence
refits, and leaves its native-1\% factor-25 closure upper-bound-censored.  The
separate factor-50 ceiling remediation passes only for that targeted lane and
does not re-evaluate the closure.  The regional blend also fails source
qualification and all influence gates; its zero-signal
offsets reach 7.558 mean-pull units even though none of its extraction fits
contacts factor 25.

The complete optimizer ledgers and zero exclusions establish that the
conditional computations finished under the pull-blind gate.  They do not
establish coverage or validate either generating mean.  The signals in the
closure ensembles are added only after each generator is frozen, so downstream
paired response cannot answer whether fitting that functional family to a
source containing signal would absorb it.  The dedicated influence audit
answers that question adversely.

Accordingly, both branches retain the exact phrase ``requested conditional
stress only.''  The factor-25 K2 native-1\% branch is additionally
``upper-bound-censored.''  No result in this subsection changes the accepted
v4.2 production card, its factor-15 2021 ceiling, the observed result,
confidence-limit procedure, local $p_0$, or interpretation of an observed
feature.
